# DEMAKE 86

A demake of the first game in a series that is better left unnamed. Inspired by Environmental Station Alpha, Minitroid, and a series better left unnamed.


> *"8x8 rooms, 8x8 tiles each room, and 8x8 pixels each tile. A square 8^6 is a perfect size for a game that came out in 1986."*

Seamus is the Celtic variant of the name "James". Off the east coast of Ireland are the Aran Islands. Éire is the Irish name for Ireland.
