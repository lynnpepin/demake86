ⓘ What is DEMAKE86??

Demake86, or the Legally Distinct Action Adventure Platforming Game set on a Meteoroid, is an homage to the genre-defining the 1986/8/6 release. But Demake86 is a legally distinct and totally original game.

Demake86 is a tiny adventure you can play in your browser when it releases August 6th, 2026!

THE MISSION:

You are Relan Gras, the legendary protector and sole inheritor of the ancient armaments of the now-extinct mysterious Corvus species. Your purpose is to create a true peace in space. Many believe you are a myth.

You were raised on Planet Zenith, a natural fortress planet blanketed in meteoroids. You travel the galaxy at light-speed, dedicating your life to ending intergalactic threats as they arise.

You've just received a emergency transmission: The intergalactic paramilitary bandit ring known as the Neolegion have unearthed the ancient parasitic life-form known as SETONA, discovered in the labyrinthine fortress-ruins of the largest Meteoroid in the orbit of Zenith.

The transmission suggests the Neolegion base is hosted by their ringleader, the mechanical life-vein known as the Margaret Baroness. She is joined by the corpulent cave-dwelling monster Kemp and Relan's personal nemesis, the plasma-breathing dragon and genocidier Reagan.

If they were to tame the Setona, the shadow of the imperial rule of the Neolegion would engulf all intergalactic life. You never expected to return to your homeworld, let alone to quell the sins of your ancestors.
